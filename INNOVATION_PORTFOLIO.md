## Project 1: Personalized Skincare & Professional Consultation Platform

### The Problem

Many people struggle to find skincare products suitable for their individual skin needs. A person may spend money trying different products without knowing what is appropriate for their skin characteristics, concerns, sensitivities, or different parts of their body. Some people visit a skin professional mainly to get guidance on which products are suitable for them.

### Proposed Solution

A digital platform that allows users to build a personal skin profile and, where appropriate, connect with qualified skincare professionals for personalized guidance.

### Planned Features

- Personal skin profile with separate information for different body areas
- Product history tracking
- User-reported reactions and sensitivities
- Ingredient information and compatibility checking
- Verified product database
- AI-assisted organization of user information
- Professional review and consultation
- Personalized skincare routines
- Progress and feedback tracking

### Expertise Needed

`Dermatology / skin-health professionals` · `Software developers` · `UI/UX designers` · `Cybersecurity & privacy specialists` · `Legal & regulatory advisors`

## Project 2: Kid-Friendly Safety and Tracking System

### The Problem

Parents and guardians worry about the safety of children, particularly during travel between home, school, and other approved locations. Existing options are often either too intrusive or don't give guardians a reliable, timely way to know a child is safe.

### Proposed Solution

A privacy-conscious safety system that helps authorized guardians monitor safety and receive alerts when necessary — without becoming constant surveillance.

### Planned Features

- Authorized guardian access controls
- Safety alerts and emergency communication
- Location-based safety zones (home, school)
- Secure management of authorized contacts

### Key Considerations

Because this handles children's location data, the system requires strong:

- Privacy protection and data protection
- Cybersecurity architecture
- Consent procedures
- Child safeguarding practices

### Expertise Needed

`Software developers` · `Cybersecurity specialists` · `Privacy professionals` · `Child safeguarding experts` · `Legal advisors`

## Project 3: Location-Based Attendance System

### The Problem

Organizations need a more reliable way to confirm that employees are physically present at an approved work location when recording attendance.

### Proposed Solution

A web or mobile-based attendance system that uses location verification (latitude/longitude) to support clock-in and clock-out processes.

### Planned Features

- Employee registration
- Clock in / clock out with location verification
- Approved workplace location management
- Attendance records and reporting
- Administrative dashboard

### Potential Users

Schools · Universities · Companies · Organizations with location-based work requirements

### Expertise Needed

`Web/mobile developers` · `Database specialists` · `Cybersecurity specialists` · `HR & operations professionals`

## Project 7: Skills-Based Training and Employment Initiative

### The Problem

Many talented people have the ability and potential to succeed but lack access to higher education because of financial or personal circumstances.

### Proposed Solution

A company or initiative that identifies and develops people based on their abilities and practical performance rather than credentials alone.

### The Model

```
Discover Potential → Train → Build Skills → Practical Assessment
    → Apprenticeship / Internship → Employment
```

### Core Principle

Opportunity should not depend only on whether someone has a degree. Potential, skill, discipline, and willingness to learn should also matter.

### Possible Training Areas

Technology · Cybersecurity · Software development · Digital skills · Business operations · Customer service
